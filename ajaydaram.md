### 🏛 About Me
I am a Software Developer and **MAICS** student at the intersection of **Baptist theology** and **Digital Humanities**. My work is centered on digitizing historical thought and creating distraction-free digital sanctuaries for the modern reader.

- 🎓 **Academic:** Pursuing an M.A. in Intercultural Studies with a focus on Baptist theology.
- 📖 **Research:** 17th-century allegorical literature and Grandhika-style Telugu translations.
- 🛠 **Philosophy:** Minimalist, sleek, and high-performance interfaces.

---

### 💻 Featured Projects
- **Scriptorium (Selah):** A web-based chronological reading application designed for deep engagement with historical and theological texts. Built as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
- **Grandhika Digitization:** Converting classical literary styles into modern, accessible JSON formats for digital preservation.
- **Theological Tooling:** Building specialized interfaces for distraction-free research and distraction-free learning.
